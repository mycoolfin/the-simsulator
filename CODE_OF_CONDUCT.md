# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the community

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Spam or off-topic content
- Sharing others' private information

## Enforcement

Violations may result in temporary or permanent bans from the project. Report issues to the project maintainers or via [Discord](https://discord.gg/ygxhStrE).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces including GitHub, Discord, and other community channels.
